# Roadmap

This roadmap is directional rather than a promise of dates.

## Near term

- Test current ComfyUI stable and nightly releases.
- Ship an example image-to-video workflow in `example_workflows/`.
- Add pagination and keyboard navigation to the gallery.
- Expand tests around routes, moves, undo, and video metadata.

## Later

- User-defined source-node matching rules.
- Multiple named libraries and independent ledgers.
- Ledger export/import and integrity checks.
- Additional community translations.

Features that preserve local-only operation and non-destructive defaults are preferred.
